# SIGNBOARD STUDIO — IMMERSIVE DIGITAL PORTFOLIO

> *"We make businesses impossible to ignore."*

World-class, immersive digital portfolio for **SIGNBOARD STUDIO**, a founder-led creative digital studio in Hyderabad, India.

---

## ✦ Brand Identity & Palette
- **Deep Red:** `#B5121B`
- **Near Black:** `#111111` / `#0A0A0A`
- **Burnt Orange:** `#F06A28`
- **Typography:** High-contrast editorial display serif paired with clean modern geometric sans-serif and monospace technical accents.

## ✦ Core Experience & Sections
1. **Cinematic Opening Experience (`IntroSequence`):**
   - SVG line drawing reveal of the official Signboard 'S' monogram
   - Ambient red/orange volumetric lighting flare
   - Morph into the complete `SIGNBOARD STUDIO` wordmark
   - Headline: *"WE MAKE BUSINESSES IMPOSSIBLE TO IGNORE."*
   - Supporting text: *"Creative digital experiences. Powerful visual storytelling. Intelligent solutions."*
   - Interactive CTAs: `EXPLORE OUR WORLD` and `START A PROJECT`
   - Accessible `SKIP INTRO [ESC]`

2. **Section A — Immersive Hero:**
   - Full-screen atmosphere with reactive particle canvas
   - Editorial headline and magnetic CTAs
   - Studio telemetry and live IST clock

3. **Section B — Brand Introduction:**
   - Founder-led studio ethos & guiding principle
   - 4-pillar manifesto (Architectural Distinction, Kinetic Storytelling, Intelligent Technology, Radical Visibility)

4. **Section C — Services (Interactive Labs):**
   - **Web Design & Development:** Interactive code inspector & 100/100 Core Web Vitals
   - **Video Editing & Motion Content:** Interactive aspect-ratio switcher (16:9 Cinema, 9:16 Reel, 1:1 Feed)
   - **Photography & Visual Creative:** Interactive lighting and color grade switcher (Cinematic, Editorial, Noir)
   - **AI-Powered Digital Solutions:** Interactive neural workflow pipeline simulation

5. **Section D — Selected Work & Case Studies:**
   - Curated archive with distinct filter categories
   - High-fidelity interactive full-screen case-study modals
   - Transparent, authentic labeling of concept studies (`CONCEPT WORK`)

6. **Section E — Founder Profile (Suhith):**
   - Profile of Suhith (Founder, Creative Director & Full-Stack Technologist)
   - Philosophy, development background, verified skill matrix

7. **Section F — Our Process:**
   - 5-phase interactive workflow: `01 Discover` → `02 Strategy` → `03 Create` → `04 Develop` → `05 Launch`
   - Detailed phase deliverables and timelines

8. **Section G — Contact & Conversion:**
   - Headline: *"LET'S CREATE SOMETHING THAT GETS REMEMBERED."*
   - Full inquiry form with discipline pills, budget selector, and validation
   - Official Instagram: `@signboard_studio`

9. **Section H — Footer:**
   - Official logo, navigation directory, social channels, copyright, magnetic back-to-top trigger
